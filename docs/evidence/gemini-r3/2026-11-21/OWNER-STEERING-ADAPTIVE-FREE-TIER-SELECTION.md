5. **Scoped failures:** 429 short throttle vs per-model/feature RPD quota vs positively project-wide exhaustion; no project-wide disable from an ambiguous Google `quota_exceeded`. 503 model overload: short circuit/cooldown that *exact model* while other free alternatives remain eligible. 6. **Truthful capacities:** parent `smart-nt` may advertise up to 262,144 only when some eligible physical target actually fits; Gemma's advertised architectural 262,144 cannot bypass 16K input TPM.
